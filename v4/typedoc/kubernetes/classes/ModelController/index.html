ModelController

@webda/kubernetes

Class: ModelController

Defined in: kubernetes/src/operator/controller.ts:118

Reconcile the objects of one model stored in a KubernetesStore

It watches the resources of the model, queues the objects whose spec changed (metadata.generation), runs their reconcile and writes status.observedGeneration and a Ready condition. Status-only changes, its own status writes included, are not reconciled again.
