StreamingOperationContext

@webda/core

Abstract Class: StreamingOperationContext

Defined in: packages/core/src/contexts/streamingcontext.ts:30

The shared part of a context serving a streaming operation over a transport (gRPC, WebSocket, GraphQL subscription, ...):

  • a non-streamed result is buffered as usual (writeResult())
  • a streamed chunk is sent right away (sendChunk()), write() returning false under backpressure
  • drained() waits for the transport (waitForDrain()) without leaving listeners behind: the signal it gets is aborted once the wait is over
  • once cancelled (the client went away) or once the connection is over (connectionEnded), write() and drained() throw OperationCancelledError

The transport calls cancel() when the client leaves, and also ends the operation's input queue so its pending read returns and its finally blocks run.
